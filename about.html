Our Story

Crafting Memories Beyond Ordinary Tourism

Founded by a team of veteran luxury hoteliers and private aviators, Aura was born out of a desire to eliminate the friction from luxury travel. We realized that true luxury is not just a five-star hotel room; it is time well spent, private access that money alone cannot buy, and the peace of mind knowing that every detail is perfected.

Today, our global network spans private atolls in the Maldives, royal suites in the United Arab Emirates, historic chalets in the Swiss Alps, and expedition river cruisers along the ancient Nile.
